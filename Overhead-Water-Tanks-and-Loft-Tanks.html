Overhead Water Tanks and Loft Tanks

Range offers premium quality overhead and loft water tanks made from 100% virgin, food-grade material, ensuring safety for potable water. Available in 100 to 10,000 litres, these tanks come in 2, 3, and 4-layered options with UV-stable, thermally insulated designs to maintain water temperature. Their unique ribbed structure adds superior strength and impact resistance, preventing bulging and enhancing durability. SILTANK tanks are easy to install with built-in lifting hooks and feature strong, threaded covers. Rigorously tested and BIS-compliant, they guarantee full capacity, long-lasting performance, and hygienic water storage—making them a reliable and value-for-money solution.
